The Mudita Project

Health that spreads.

Mudita is the old Sanskrit word for gladness at other people's wellbeing. This is a hub for body, behavior, and mind, built on one idea: what helps you should be easy to pass on.

The idea

Most wellness advice ends at you.

Advice about sleep, habits, and stress usually stops with the person reading it. Mudita keeps going. It is the practice of feeling real gladness when someone around you is doing well, and it sits beside kindness, compassion, and balance as one of the four boundless states in Buddhist teaching. Patanjali lists the same four in Yoga Sutra 1.33.

The Mudita Project puts that idea to work. Every part of the hub asks two questions: what helps me, and how do I hand this to someone else?

How it spreads

Four moves, repeated.

  1. Notice

    Catch one thing that made your week easier.

  2. Practice

    Do it long enough that it becomes yours.

  3. Share

    Tell one person, plainly, with no pitch attached.

  4. Return

    Ask how it went for them, and be glad when it works.
